## 🔴 ACCOUNTING & TAXATION PRIORITIES

### Ghanaian Tax Compliance (Critical - Phase 2)
- **VAT Management**: 15% VAT calculation, tracking, and reporting
- **NHIL**: 2.5% National Health Insurance Levy
- **GETFund**: 2.5% Ghana Education Trust Fund
- **Tourism Levy**: 1% on hospitality services
- **GRA Reporting**: Automated tax return generation
- **Tax Payment Tracking**: Complete audit trail for all tax payments

### Financial Management Features
- **Multi-currency Support**: GHS primary, USD/EUR secondary
- **Cost Center Management**: Department-wise cost allocation
- **Budget Planning**: Annual and monthly budget management
- **Profitability Analysis**: Room, F&B, and service profitability
- **Cash Flow Management**: Real-time cash position tracking

### Compliance & Reporting
- **GRA Audit Ready**: Complete documentation and audit trails
- **Financial Statements**: Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow
- **Tax Returns**: Automated generation and submission
- **Cost Analysis**: COGS, operational costs, and variance analysis
- **Performance Metrics**: Financial KPIs and benchmarking
