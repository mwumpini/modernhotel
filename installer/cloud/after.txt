AGM Sync on this computer is linked to the cloud.

Close any other AGM Sync window first, then use this icon.
The first page asks for the hotel code and the cloud sign-in.
After that, people at home and people in the hotel share the same records.

When the internet is down, this computer keeps working.
Changes catch up when the internet returns.
Phones and other computers on this Wi-Fi open the address on the sign-in page.
