1. Sign in

  1. Open www.agmsync.com on a laptop (Chrome or Edge).
  2. Organization ID: demo
  3. Email and password: the ones you were sent. Do not share them.
  4. If the page looks old after an update, press Ctrl + F5.

2. A few rules

  • Type your first name at the top. The guide then shows your names, for example [your name] Guest. Use exactly those names, so everyone's entries can be found and compared.
  • One of each: one guest, one event, one supplier, one employee. Do not make extra.
  • Use the same amounts as this guide. Everyone should then see the same results; a different result is a problem worth reporting.
  • Everything is already set up: taxes, rooms, rates, departments. You only do the day-to-day work.
  • Use any room that shows available. If another tester takes it first, pick another.
  • Do not run Night Audit, close a period or year, approve a payroll month, or change System Settings. They affect everyone.
  • If something waits for approval, note it and carry on.
  • Press ✅ or ❌ under each task. For ❌, write what happened. This browser remembers your ticks.
  • At the end, press Copy my results and paste them into WhatsApp.

Taxes: rooms and events have 21% added on top (VAT 15%, NHIL 2.5%, GETFund 2.5%, Tourism Levy 1%): ₵100 becomes ₵121. Your menu prices include the tax: ₵121 on the menu is ₵100 + ₵21 tax.

3. Your tasks

Do them in order: later tasks use what you made earlier. Getting lost is useful feedback too, so note it.

🛎️ Front Office

A guest arrives, stays, and later leaves (you check them out in F5).

F1

Your guest

  1. Front Office Operations → Clients → New Client: [your name] Guest, a person, with any phone number.
Check: [your name] Guest is in the client list.
F2

Walk-in for one night

  1. Front Office Operations → Desk → Walk-in: guest [your name] Guest, any room that shows available, leaving tomorrow.
  2. Write down the room number and the nightly rate.
  3. Take cash for the room (rate + 21% tax), then Check in.
Check: The folio shows one night: rate + 21% tax (₵200 becomes ₵242). Paid = the same amount. Outstanding ₵0.00. The room shows occupied.
F3

A booking that is cancelled

  1. Desk → Reservations → New Reservation: [your name] Guest, any available room, arriving in 7 days, one night. No payment.
  2. Open it and cancel it (reason: guest called to cancel).
Check: It shows cancelled. Any fee shown follows the hotel's cancellation policy (write down what it said).

Words you will see

FolioA guest's bill: room nights, meals charged to the room, and payments.
Walk-inA guest who arrives without a booking.
TillOne cashier's cash drawer for a shift. It must be open before taking cash.
KOTKitchen order ticket, sent when an order is sent to the kitchen.
RecipeWhat one dish uses from stock. Selling the dish takes those amounts off stock.
PO / GRNPurchase order (what we ask for) / goods received note (what arrived).
Requisition / IssueA department asking the store for stock / the store giving it.
Goods Received, Not InvoicedStock that has arrived but whose bill is not entered yet. The bill with the GRN number clears it.
Payable / ReceivableMoney the hotel owes suppliers / money customers owe the hotel.
JournalEvery accounting entry, each with equal debits and credits.
SSNIT / PAYESocial security (5.5% of basic from the employee) / income tax taken from salary.
