The back office from start to finish: buying and receiving stock, the supplier's bill and payment, how it all reaches the accounts, and one employee through payroll.

⏱ about 2 to 2.5 hours📦 Inventory → 🧾 Accounting → 👥 Payroll🧾 18 small tasks

1. Sign in

  1. Open www.agmsync.com on a laptop (Chrome or Edge). These screens are easier on a big screen.
  2. Organization ID: demo
  3. Email and password: the ones Abdul sent you. Do not share them.
  4. If the page looks old after an update, press Ctrl + F5.

2. A few rules

  • Everyone uses the same practice hotel (Demo Hotel). Use your own name on every supplier, item or employee you create, for example Gina Fresh Farms. That way nobody mixes up whose is whose.
  • Do not run Night Audit. It moves the whole hotel to the next day.
  • Do not approve a payroll month without asking Abdul first (task G16).
  • Nothing here is real money. Try things, even "wrong" things. Finding problems is the point.
  • Each task has a ✅ check. Tick it if it worked, or mark ❌ and write down what happened.
  • Write down your PO number (G3) and GRN number (G4). You need them in Part B.

3. Your tasks

Do them in order: each part uses what you made in the one before. Getting lost is useful feedback too, so note it.

Part B · Accounting & Finance

How the stock you just moved reaches the books, and paying the supplier.

G8

The delivery in the books

  1. Accounting & Finance → 📒 Books → Journal: find the entry for your GRN number.
Check: Stock (Food & Beverage) goes up ₵500 and Goods Received, Not Invoiced shows ₵500. The supplier is not owed yet, because there is no bill.
Notes:
G9

Enter the supplier's bill

  1. Accounting & Finance → 🧾 Payable → New bill: supplier Gina Fresh Farms, amount 500, plus VAT if the form offers it.
  2. Fill Delivery / GRN # with your GRN number (or Our PO # with your PO number).
  3. Press Approve & Post.
Check: In Who we owe, Gina Fresh Farms is owed the bill total once (₵500, or ₵500 + tax). Not ₵1,000.
Notes:
G10

The bill in the books

  1. Books → Journal: open the entry for your bill.
Check: It clears Goods Received, Not Invoiced (₵500), puts any VAT in input tax, and records what is owed to the supplier. Stock is not added a second time.
Notes:
G11

Pay the supplier

  1. Payable: open your bill → Record payment, full amount, by bank transfer.
Check: The bill shows Paid and Gina Fresh Farms owes nothing in Who we owe.
Notes:
G12

The kitchen issue and the count

  1. Books → Journal: find the entries for your issue (G5) and your count (G6).
Check: The issue moves ₵100 from stock to a kitchen cost. The count posts a ₵10 stock loss.
Notes:
G13

The statements

  1. Accounting & Finance → 📑 Statements: open Trial Balance, then Balance Sheet, then Income Statement.
Check: Trial Balance: total debits = total credits. Balance Sheet: assets = equity + liabilities. Your ₵100 kitchen cost and ₵10 loss appear as costs.
Notes:
G14

Who owes us (look only)

  1. Accounting & Finance → 📝 Receivable: look at Who owes us and the Doubtful debts tab. Do not post anything here.
Check: The ageing columns (Current, 1-30, 31-60…) make sense to you. Note anything unclear.

Words you will see

POPurchase order: what we ask a supplier for.
GRNGoods received note: what actually arrived.
Goods Received, Not InvoicedStock that has arrived but whose bill has not been entered yet. Entering the bill with the GRN or PO number clears it, so the supplier is owed only once.
IssueGiving stock from the store to a department (it becomes that department's cost).
Stock countCounting what is really on the shelf; any difference is posted as a gain or loss.
Payable / ReceivableMoney the hotel owes suppliers / money customers owe the hotel.
JournalThe list of every accounting entry, each with equal debits and credits.
Trial BalanceAll accounts with their totals; debits must equal credits.
Input VATVAT paid on purchases, which the hotel can take off the VAT it owes GRA.
SSNITSocial security: 5.5% of basic salary from the employee; the hotel pays its own share on top.
PAYEIncome tax taken from salary and paid to GRA.
PayslipThe employee's pay statement for the month.
