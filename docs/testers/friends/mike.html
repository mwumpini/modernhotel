A full hotel day: bookings, check-in, the room bill, the restaurant, housekeeping, stock, and closing the day.

⏱ about 2 to 3 hours🛎️ Rooms 101, 102, 201, 202🧾 12 small tasks

1. Sign in

  1. Open www.agmsync.com on a laptop (Chrome or Edge). A phone works for the restaurant and housekeeping screens.
  2. Organization ID: demo
  3. Email and password: the ones Abdul sent you. Do not share them.
  4. If the page looks old after an update, press Ctrl + F5.

2. A few rules

  • Everyone uses the same practice hotel (Demo Hotel). Use your own name on every guest, client or item you create, for example Mike Test Guest. That way nobody mixes up whose is whose.
  • Only use your rooms (listed in your guide). Other rooms belong to the other testers.
  • Do not run Night Audit unless your guide says so. It moves the whole hotel to the next day.
  • Nothing here is real money. Try things, even "wrong" things. Finding problems is the point.
  • Each task has a ✅ check. Tick it if it worked, or mark ❌ and write down what happened.

Taxes: room and event prices have tax added on top (VAT 15%, NHIL 2.5%, GETFund 2.5%, Tourism Levy 1%). Menu prices already include tax: the guest pays the menu price and the tax is shown inside it. Bills list the taxes as NHIL/GETFund 5%, VAT 15%, Tourism Levy 1%.

4. When something goes wrong

Write down:

  1. The task number (for example M4).
  2. The screen: the menu on the left and the tab at the top.
  3. What you typed or clicked.
  4. What you expected, and what actually happened.
  5. A phone photo or screenshot of the screen, if you can.

Then carry on with the next task. Send your notes and photos to Abdul on WhatsApp.

5. At the end, tell us

  1. Which screen was the easiest to use?
  2. Which screen was the most confusing, and why?
  3. Was any word or button name unclear? Which one?
  4. Was anything slow?
  5. What would you add or change before a real hotel uses this?

Words you will see

FolioA guest's bill: room nights, restaurant charges, and payments.
In houseA guest who has checked in and not left yet.
Walk-inA guest who arrives without a booking.
Night auditThe end-of-day close. It adds the next room night to every guest and moves the hotel to the next day.
TillOne cashier's cash drawer for a shift. It must be open before taking cash.
KOT / BOTKitchen / bar order ticket, printed when an order is sent.
RecipeWhat one dish uses from stock. Selling the dish takes those amounts off stock.
RequisitionA department asking the store for stock.
PO / GRNPurchase order (what we ask a supplier for) / goods received note (what arrived).
Receivable / PayableMoney customers owe the hotel / money the hotel owes suppliers.
